Your calendar, planned for you.

Lumina is an AI calendar. It connects to your calendar, email, chat apps and to-do lists, then plans your week around what actually needs doing, and protects your free time.

What Lumina does

  • Shows your meetings and the free time between them.
  • Finds tasks and meeting requests in your email and chat.
  • Proposes a plan for your week: focus blocks, task time and protected free time.
  • Drafts reschedule emails when a meeting needs to move. Nothing is sent or changed without your approval.

Google data

When you connect Google, Lumina reads your Google Calendar events and the list of your calendars, and reads your Gmail inbox to find tasks and meeting requests. Read the privacy policy for exactly what is collected, how it is used and how to delete it.

Lumina is in a private beta.
