Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Lumina ("we") is an AI calendar in private beta. This policy explains what data Lumina collects, why, and the choices you have. In short: we only use your data to run Lumina for you, we never sell it, and we never use it for advertising.

Data we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and profile picture from Google when you sign in.
  • Google Calendar: the list of your calendars, and event details (title, time, location, attendees, meeting link, your response status) for a rolling window of recent and upcoming events.
  • Gmail: for messages in your inbox, the sender, subject, date, a short preview and a link back to the message. Full message bodies are read only when Lumina analyzes a message for tasks or meeting requests, and are not kept afterwards.
  • Your preferences: working hours, focus time, quiet hours and similar settings you enter.
  • Activity records: a log of actions Lumina takes for you, such as notifications sent, so you can see what happened.

How we use it

Only to provide Lumina's features to you: showing your schedule, finding tasks and meeting requests, proposing plans, and drafting messages for your approval. Lumina never sends email or changes an event that involves other people without your explicit approval.

Google API data

Lumina's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, we:

  • use Google user data only to provide and improve user-facing features of Lumina;
  • do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising;
  • do not allow humans to read your Google data, except with your consent, for security purposes, to comply with the law, or when aggregated and anonymized;
  • do not use Google user data to train general-purpose AI or machine learning models.

AI processing

To find tasks and plan your week, Lumina sends relevant parts of your messages and calendar to an AI model provider for processing. We only use providers whose terms prohibit training their models on that data, and the results are used only to provide Lumina's features to you.

Storage and security

  • Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database and transmitted over encrypted connections.
  • Access tokens for connected services are encrypted at rest.
  • Lumina only requests the access it needs, and asks for more only when a feature needs it.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run Lumina: database hosting, application hosting, background job processing and AI model processing. They process data only on our behalf to run the service.

Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept while your account is active and deleted when you delete your account.
  • You can disconnect Google at any time in Lumina's settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Lumina then stops accessing your Google data.
  • To delete your account and all associated data, email us at the address below. We will complete deletion within 30 days.

Children

Lumina is not intended for anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
